SCORE
Z = mean(g - p_hat) over the earlier eligible pair-excluded scoring history.
The upstream score is already stored. This audit does not reconstruct it.
Raw historical grant rate = adjusted_7d + expected_7d.
Historical grant count = n_7d * raw rate, required within 1e-6 of an integer.
Zero-grant history means this count is exactly zero, not zero adjusted Z.
It does not mean lifetime never-granted, and does not describe a latent type.
The fixed reference SD is 0.059411690344000595, inherited from the earlier
first-exposure fit. It is not the SD of unique opponents' raw grant rates.

POPULATIONS
All populations refer to supported opponents appearing in the chosen cohort.
They do not cover every opponent with a supported score anywhere on Lichess.
1. exposure_weighted: each exposure has unit weight.
2. equal_opponent_weight_across_exposures: weight=1/count(exposures of opponent).
   Each opponent has total weight one, spread over their observed histories.
3. one_snapshot_per_opponent: one observation for each opponent, chosen at their
   earliest eligible exposure. A timestamp tie breaks by the frozen row_id.
Scores can differ by month, excluded recipient and cross-fitting reference.
Quantiles use the inverse weighted empirical CDF, without interpolation.
Histograms use 60 equal-width bins over full observed Z support and [0,1] for
the raw rate. No tail observations are discarded. CSVs include all three
populations; the plots display exposure weights and the first opponent snapshot.

SCORE-BAND FIRST STAGE
Default: ten exposure-quantile bands of raw historical Z. Identical scores are
not split; empty bins are dropped and the actual number of bins is reported.
Use --bands 5 for quintiles. No data-driven choice of band count is performed.

The original first stage is D = beta*Z + X*gamma + additive FEs + error.
X and the FEs come from the unchanged, vendored expanded_stats.controls(d,True).
The 15 continuous controls are recipient rating; rating gap, squared gap and
absolute gap; log(1+opponent prior decisions); log(1+prior counterparts);
expected historical rate; recipient fair-history indicator, log fair count,
ever-kind indicator, kind rate, log disconnect count, log receipt count,
log prior 30-day activity and log prior 90-day claim count.
FEs are assignment cell (month, base seconds, increment seconds, floor(mean
pair rating/100)), exposure date and hour of week. All transformations and
missing-value rules are inherited from the original source.

Before new results are accepted, the linear first stage, its clustered SE and
the residual-score SD must reproduce the saved cohort fit within 2e-8 in
native units. Original slope 1.1633383003440598; SE .00666888594691755;
native residual-score SD .057367422080685834. Run 3 uses its saved fit.

Raw curve: mean D in each Z band.
Adjusted curve: replace continuous Z with K-1 band indicators, retaining X/FEs.
If b is that fitted vector, Bbar its full-cohort mean and e_k the band's dummy
vector (all zeros for band 1), adjusted mean_k = Dbar + (e_k - Bbar)'b.
These standardize every band to the same empirical X/FE distribution.
They are not raw probabilities and may lie outside [0,1]; no clipping occurs.
Linear comparison: Dbar + beta*(mean Z in band - overall mean Z).

Uncertainty: the upstream shared-account OR exposure-date CR0 estimator, with
pointwise normal 95% intervals. The band-model centered intercept and band
coefficients are propagated jointly. Historical scores, covariates and bin
cutpoints are treated as fixed; generated-score uncertainty is not propagated.
Nonpositive or non-PSD covariance is explicitly unavailable, never repaired.

Nested linearity diagnostic: add all K-1 band indicators to continuous Z and
X/FEs, then test their coefficients jointly equal to zero with the same robust
covariance and asymptotic chi-square reference. This detects step departures;
it is not a comprehensive test of every nonlinear alternative. Failure to reject
does not establish exact linearity. Report plot, departure magnitudes and p-value.
Neither this test nor positive average band associations establishes individual
monotonicity or the IV exclusion restriction. This is not an FLL test.

PROFILE CACHE
Bounded discovery: up to 45 seconds / 15,000 directories / depth eight; inspect
at most 250 candidate files. Inspect Parquet schemas and at most 256 records,
CSV/TSV headers and records, and bounded JSON/JSONL samples. No network requests.
The result distinguishes nonempty values found, schema only, and not found in
this bounded search. A negative result does not prove the field was never saved.
Use --profile-path PATH to inspect a specific file or folder if discovery misses it.
Only field names and aggregate availability are exported. Private input paths
stay in the local audit directory. No account IDs or profile values are exported.
Field availability alone does not validate the identity join or temporal status.
A current self-reported profile field is not automatically a pre-exposure balance
covariate. No country-balance regression is silently constructed here.

VALIDATION
Offline tests compare the band regression and its covariance with explicit
full-dummy OLS, compare shared-account/date covariance with an explicit adjacency
matrix, check denominators and history recovery, preserve tied scores, reject
corrupted input hashes, inspect nested profile fields without exporting values,
and exercise aggregate report creation and checkpoint reuse with synthetic data.
Real results are produced only after the package runs on the verified local data.
