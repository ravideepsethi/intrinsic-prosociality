Specified follow-up on October 5, 2026, after examining the reputation report.
Not a preregistration before the original research or earlier results.

WHY THIS EXTENSION
The previous index sample was pool/nonadjacent, but its later-meeting search
used all-source rated chronology. It therefore included later rated challenges
and rematches in total meeting counts. It omitted casual games, did not split
later games by source, and did not measure follow-link formation. Its rating
repayment accounting also omitted the possible benefit of a social connection.

SELECTION AND COMPARISON
Use the authenticated parent's 8,300,865 opportunities. Restrict to first
recorded rated meetings and anchors with full 180 days before November 2025.
The parent chronology starts in January 2013; first recorded meeting does not
mean first personal contact. A pair can have casual or off-platform history.
One earliest eligible row per unordered pair is retained, irrespective of
granting and future contact. Author accounts are already excluded.

Cells are chooser ID x calendar quarter x speed x evaluation band, from the
disconnected player's viewpoint: <=-300, (-300,-100), [-100,100), [100,300),
and >=300 centipawns. A cell needs at least one grant and one claim. Within
each cell, the lowest MD5 of game ID plus a fixed salt selects each arm.
Another salted MD5 ranks cells. Increasing --max-sets adds cells to the same
ranking; per-query API caches remain reusable across runs. One observation
per arm per cell avoids weighting large cells more heavily. This is a
restricted overlap population, and may overrepresent more active choosers.
The grant selection and comparison are not a random assignment of kindness.

TIMING AND COVERAGE
Pregame window: [start-90 days,start). Postgame windows: (lastMoveAt,lastMoveAt+H].
lastMoveAt is a proxy for the decision endpoint. Sensitivities begin one hour
or seven days after it. Windows use UTC elapsed time. Distinct dates are UTC
dates and not a count of sessions. Seven-day span needs at least two contacts
separated by at least seven elapsed days.

The API query has a one-day buffer at both ends. Actual inclusion uses each
game's createdAt. Parent PGN-derived starts have one-second precision, so
validation compares floor(createdAt/1000) to floor(parent start/1000).
Focal lastMoveAt must agree exactly. Duplicate identical game IDs are removed;
conflicting duplicates make that history unavailable. Every expected rated
game ID must be retrieved with matching time and rated flag. A focal-only
response is accepted only when no other rated games are expected. Unknown
casual games cannot be used for an independent completeness test.

Missing/closed/restricted histories, malformed responses, failed identity
checks, or missing expected rated games remain unavailable. A systemic failure
of the first twenty histories stops the network pass and exports diagnostics.
There are at most six retries for transient transport failures, with rate-
limit backoff. Successful complete requests are cached and never refetched
by a normal rerun. No max-games cap or pagination truncation is used.

GAME SOURCE
All source types enter total and casual/rated outcomes except imported/relay
records. Non-pool means a returned, nonempty source label unequal to pool.
Missing source labels give unknown category membership, not a non-pool zero.
Source-specific outcomes include friend, lobby, organized (tournament/swiss/
simul), and pool. The friend label is not a friendship edge. Unrecognized but
nonempty source labels count as non-pool and are visible in GAME_SOURCE_COUNTS.
Repeated non-pool play is a proxy for an ongoing connection, not proof of one.
Unsuccessful or declined challenges are not observed in completed-game exports.

MISSING-DATA BOUNDS
For binary outcomes, each event has a lower and upper indicator. An unavailable
history is [0,1]. Unknown-source games widen source-specific indicators when
they could change membership. One known qualifying game fixes the any-contact
indicator at 1 even if other source labels are absent.
Arm-level rates average these bounds across the entire selected roster.
The full-roster difference bounds are mean(grant lower - claim upper) and
mean(grant upper - claim lower). These are identification bounds for observed
missingness, not confidence intervals. They do not bound invisible API games.
Matched fits require both outcomes to be known; their usable N is explicit.
The secondary no-pre90-contact cohort requires both successfully exported
histories to show no joint completed games in the preceding 90 days. This is
a pre-event restriction, not a restriction on realized future contact.

ESTIMATORS
Each matched set provides a grant-minus-claim difference in later contact.
Raw comparisons are the mean of these differences. Adjusted comparisons are
the intercept from a regression of differences on differences in covariates,
equivalent to a linear outcome regression with matched-set fixed effects.
Covariates: evaluation clipped to +/-2000 cp, both ratings and RDs, win premium,
draw payoff, calendar day, ply, both prior-30-day playing volumes, time-control
base/increment, and log clocks plus missing-clock indicators. Post-event fits
also control for the pre-90-day any-contact and casual-contact indicators.
These controls are descriptive; selection on unmeasured recipient or game
attributes can remain. A higher contact rate would not establish whether
future friendship was anticipated by the original giver.

Standard errors cluster by chooser, allowing dependency among cells for the
same chooser, with G/(G-1) correction and normal pointwise intervals. Residual
dependence through shared recipients or calendar shocks is not additionally
modeled. No unsupported zero-variance interval is presented as a precise null.
Controls with no variance or redundant columns are removed without changing
their span. Nonfinite required controls are excluded in adjusted fits.
Holm adjustment covers three adjusted main outcomes in all matched sets:
any non-pool contact in 90 days; any casual contact in 90 days; non-pool contact
on at least two UTC dates in 90 days. Unsupported members retain their place
in the family of three. Other windows, categories, and cohorts are diagnostics.

INTERPRETATION
An increase in deliberate or sustained contact would identify a plausible
social return that rating-only accounting cannot address. An absent increase
is useful only relative to confidence intervals, API coverage, and the overlap
population. Neither result identifies anticipated reputational motives or
eliminates immediate recipient approval, private messages, spectators, or
unobserved social interaction. Formal follow-link formation remains unmeasured.
