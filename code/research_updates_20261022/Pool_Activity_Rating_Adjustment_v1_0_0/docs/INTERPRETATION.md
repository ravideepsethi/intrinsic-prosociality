# What this comparison can establish

The current equal-chooser activity comparison has competitive/worse rates of 4.097%/0.907% for light choosers and 2.659%/0.413% for heavy choosers. The pp gap is smaller among heavy choosers but the rate ratio is larger: 6.447 versus 4.517, a heavy/light ratio of 1.427 (95% CI [1.390,1.466]).

Raw current-pool rating-tier tables show a related pattern: competitive/worse ratios of 5.18 below 1600, 8.48 at 1600–1999, 8.54 at 2000–2399, and 10.19 at 2400+. Those opportunity-level rating tables alone do not establish a correlation between activity and rating and cannot supply an adjusted activity estimate.

Read the new output in this order:

1. Check mean and median rating, rating distributions and correlations by activity. The guessed positive relationship may or may not hold for qualifying disconnection opportunities.
2. Check common-support retention. Compare the full-sample raw ratio of RRs with the common-support raw ratio before attributing any change to rating adjustment.
3. Compare the common-support raw, rating-standardized, and rating-plus-time-control-standardized ratios of RRs. These use the same people. Inspect the underlying state rates and absolute gaps as well.
4. Use the paired change intervals to assess whether adjustment materially changes the contrast. Do not compare separate significance stars or assume raw and adjusted estimates are independent.

A movement of the heavy/light ratio of RRs toward one is consistent with these measured composition differences accounting for part of the proportional association. A remaining contrast describes conditional heterogeneity. Crossing one, becoming imprecise, or showing little change should be reported as observed, without treating a nonsignificant result as equivalence.

The covariates are chooser summaries over the observed panel: mean pre-game rating in the modal observed time control, with rating bands. They are not randomized or fixed before the panel. Adjustment does not identify an effect of activity, a moral type, or an individual preference parameter. It does not fully remove within-band rating variation or differences in focal-game circumstances.

The original minimal FE estimate remains 2.1745 pp and its equal-chooser counterpart remains 2.8202 pp. This add-on supplements those estimates and does not reestimate any of the paper's other adjusted models. Zero-grant choosers are retained throughout wherever covariate support permits comparison.
