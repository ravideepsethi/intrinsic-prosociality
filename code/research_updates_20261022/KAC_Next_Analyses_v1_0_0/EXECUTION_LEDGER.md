## Corrections to the handoff's decision rules

- A1 establishes a conditional profile. Engine advantage and material do not identify perceived desert or a motive. The fitted central curve rises, but forced mate and the finite ≥600 tail are separate categories; do not claim strict monotonicity across every tail state.
- A2 proxies do not validate a zero-blame assumption simply by being insignificant. The restricted departure sample is a sensitivity, not observed accidental disconnection. Coefficient attenuation is not a causal share of blame.
- Arena points require source verification. The current official FAQ contains qualifications omitted in the handoff: later draws in a draw streak can score after the speed-dependent move threshold; a loss does not reset that draw streak; the 1+2 berserk exception also matters. These current rules do not by themselves certify rules in force during 2023–2025. Do not infer tournament end from its last observed game or berserk solely from a half clock.
- A title at a fixed displayed gap does not hold all rating consequences fixed: uncertainty and the update scale still vary. Include those controls, and retain the descriptive interpretation.
- Incident adjustment addresses measured common shocks. Residual associations do not rule out regional, device or client shocks. A later-choice-hour control is post-exposure adjustment and should be a sensitivity.
- Comparing an ordinary-loss coefficient with a receipt coefficient does not identify a share of the receipt association attributable to losing; populations and selection differ.
- A positive probability of any grant and an imprecise total-count difference can coexist. Show precision before using “sooner, not more.”
- Several current prose statements require correction from recovered sources: tenure range, Patron control set/inference/age construction, and “B4 not run.” The detailed notes are in `completed/`.
- The author's ethics determination cannot be supplied by analysis code. Final document compilation needs the actual current source, bibliography and figure set. Publication or external submission is a separate final action, after the result is concrete and reviewed.
