COUNTDOWN THRESHOLD ESTIMATION — INTERPRETATION

These are adjusted descriptive contrasts among observed active resolutions.
They do not isolate waiting selection from changed choices or reconnections.
No API requests, new engine searches, or receipt-IV refits are performed.

The specification was frozen after inspecting feasibility counts and raw rates,
before this package produced adjusted estimates. It is not a preregistration.

Primary: finite non-mate evaluation below -100 cp, material deficits 0 through 8,
unrestricted observed clocks, pre before 2025-07-19 and post from 2025-08-11.
The intervening dates are excluded. These are calendar windows; deployment time
is not verified. Two additional calendar windows are reported in full.

Main contrast: mu4 - the equal-weight linear extrapolation of mu0,mu1,mu2,mu3
to deficit 4. Its weights on deficits 0..4 are (0.5,0,-0.5,-1,1).
Checks include a quadratic extrapolation, the symmetric contrast over 2..5,
the local 2,3-to-4 extrapolation, all neighboring steps, and placebo local
contrasts at 2,3,6,7,8. Each placebo's three points are entirely on one side of
the actual cutoff. Placebos are diagnostics, not randomized counterfactuals.

Controls: evaluation 10cp-bin x stage x period fixed effects, month fixed
effects, chooser fixed effects, log1p of both last-observed clocks, rating gap
and chooser rating in hundreds of points. All effects are speed-specific;
numeric control slopes are also period-specific. Stage bands: <=10,11-20,
21-40,41-80,81+. No finite score is replaced by a capped mate value. Mate cases
are excluded from these finite-lost models and were counted in feasibility.
Iterative FE-singleton removal preserves estimable OLS slopes but changes the
population used for standardized levels; input/retained counts are reported.

Adjusted profiles average over the retained sample's other characteristics
within each speed and period. Thus levels across periods use different observed
populations. The threshold contrasts cancel the within-period level anchors.
No fitted level is truncated to [0,100]. Raw rates shown beside them refer to
the retained estimation sample, not the full feasibility population.

Primary inference: chooser-clustered CR0 covariance and normal 95% intervals.
Joint covariance matches the same chooser IDs across speeds and periods.
Profile-level uncertainty also includes sampling variation in the sample mean
and material shares used for standardization. Date-only and chooser-plus-date
CR0 checks are supplied for the main/symmetric contrasts and their changes.
The two-way formula is Vchooser+Vdate-Vchooser/date. Invalid/negative estimated
variances are flagged, not silently repaired. The date is the UTC final-move
date. Opponent IDs are absent from this feature cache, so opponent clustering
is not implemented. No inference treats deficit values as independent clusters.

Curvature table: assume the untreated adjusted profile has absolute discrete
second differences <= M percentage points per deficit squared on 0..4.
For the primary contrast the worst-case bias is 2.5*M. The table expands the
ordinary normal interval by this bound, using M=0,.01,.025,.05,.10. Bounds add
for pre/post and cross-speed differences. This is a conservative smoothness
sensitivity, not an implementation of optimized Kolesar-Rothe honest intervals.
Its validity requires the stated bound and the sampling approximation. It does
not bound confounding, selection, or bias elsewhere in the paper.

Clock sensitivities use the same last-observed-clock cuts in both periods:
bullet/ultraBullet 40s, blitz 60s, rapid 120s, classical 300s, then twice those.
These are proxies; they do not reconstruct the clock at disconnection or prove
equal time available to choose. The material counts describe the final position,
not necessarily the position at the initial disconnection.

Monthly models are fitted separately within month and speed, with chooser and
eval10-by-stage effects and the same numeric controls. Their chooser effects
therefore need not be constant over time. The pre-July equality Wald test is a
diagnostic for stable monthly contrasts, not a proof of parallel changes.

All variants and unsupported cells are reported. There is no outcome-driven
selection of knots, bins, cutoffs, or reported speeds. No multiple-testing
adjustment is applied; secondary p-values are descriptive.

Not included: broader timeout/outoftime denominator construction, exact-match
estimates, deeper engine searches, final-capture reconstruction, opponent
clustering, or a causal correction to the paper's evaluation gradient.
