# Exploratory price and reference-demand results

`elasticity_v102` retains the opportunity-cost analysis. Its positive
mean-scaled coefficient is a functional-form-sensitive conditional association,
not a causal demand elasticity.

`reference_demand_v103` treats signed draw payoff relative to the chooser's
pre-game rating as the operative reference-dependent margin. It reports the
full signed schedule, zero-reference comparisons, and separate loss/gain-side
objects. A single global signed-log elasticity is not mathematically defined.

Both modules are exploratory, outside Holm family D, and retained regardless of
sign.
