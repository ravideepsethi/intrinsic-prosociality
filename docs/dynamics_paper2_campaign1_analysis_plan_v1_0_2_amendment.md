The author has decided that Campaign 1 should be executed as soon as its frozen
support/authority requirements permit. Execution is **not** conditional on submission

The author may use Campaign 1 results in:

- the dynamics paper;
- the flagship/main intrinsic-prosociality paper; or
- both papers.

This decision is made before Campaign 1 outcome inspection.

## 2. Supersession of the execution constraint

The v1.0.0 Section 8 rule requiring the campaign to remain unexecuted until flagship
submission is superseded.

### New execution rule

Once this amendment is frozen and the already-prespecified support-only feasibility
audit has resolved the applicable support gates and authenticated input authorities,
Campaign 1 outcome estimation may begin immediately.

There is no manuscript-submission waiting period.

Modules whose required certified inputs are already available may execute without
waiting for unrelated inputs. A module whose own frozen authority is not yet certified
remains blocked only by that genuine data dependency. In particular, this amendment
does not weaken the v1.0.1 rule that confirmatory C5 uses the full 24-month profile
universe or is deferred until that authority exists.

The support-gate rules, including the C6 and C7 treatment-blind pre-outcome decisions,
remain unchanged.

---

## 3. Supersession of the flagship non-interference clause

The v1.0.0 Section 7 sentence stating that no Campaign 1 output may be cited in,
merged into, or used to reword the flagship paper is superseded.

### New cross-paper rule

Campaign 1 results may be incorporated into the flagship/main paper at the author's
discretion.

If a Campaign 1 result is used in the flagship paper:

1. its Campaign 1 provenance remains explicit;
2. its frozen epistemic label ([C], [S], or [X]) is unchanged;
3. its specification, sample, gate status, and multiple-testing treatment remain the
   ones fixed by the effective Campaign 1 plan;
4. it is not retroactively represented as a member of an earlier flagship confirmatory
   family unless a separate, explicitly dated amendment says so; and
5. inclusion in one paper does not permit omission of an unfavorable prespecified
   Campaign 1 result from the complete Campaign 1 results archive.

The existing flagship estimates and previously frozen flagship families remain
unchanged unless the author later makes a separate explicit amendment. This amendment
only permits Campaign 1 evidence to be used as additional evidence in that paper.

## 5. Reporting and provenance

All prespecified Campaign 1 analyses remain part of the complete Campaign 1 record
regardless of which paper ultimately presents them.

Any paper using a Campaign 1 result should distinguish:

- the pre-existing flagship evidence;
- the pre-outcome-planned Campaign 1 evidence; and
- any later truly post-outcome exploratory work, if such work is ever added.

This preserves the value of the pre-outcome freeze while allowing the strongest
evidence to be presented in whichever paper it best serves.
